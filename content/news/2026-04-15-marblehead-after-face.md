Great job on Sunday, guys. Marblehead is our most difficult race to run a safety net. Issues are the urban terrain, traffic, number or runners (950 on Sunday) and getting enough volunteers. All in all -- we did it! The only medicals I am aware of was minor first aid, post race, at the finish line.

Please forward your observations or suggestions for improving our safety net next year in Marblehead and/or other races.

Your volunteerism is greatly appreciated by CAARA, YuKanRun, and the race participants.
