Thank you everyone who volunteered today for the Triple Threat Half
Marathon! It may have been hot and muggy further inland, but it was a
good day for a race out on the course with cooler temperatures and a
pleasant breeze.

The race started on schedule at 8am and the last runner crossed the
finish line around 11:30.

There were no runner-related medical incidents on the course. There were
a few spectator injuries at checkpoint 3, but nothing that required
transport.

Communication went well today! Just a quick reminder that we do need to
end our communications with our FCC callsigns to meet legal
identification requirements (I mention that because I think I forgot a
couple of times).

Thanks to Jon K1TP and Tom AA1TS for ensuring that checkpoint 2 was
covered for the entire race.

We are always looking for your feedback and suggestions and we'll often
share feedback with the group if it looks like it would benefit
everyone. You can always email us at <races@caara.net>.

Kevin, K1KL & Lars, N1LKS\
CAARA Race Coordinators\
