author: N1LKS

Thank you for your help running the safety net for the Twin Lights Half
Marathon this past weekend! It was a smooth race and there were no major
communication problems. There was some wind-related excitement at the
very end of the race that may have caused me to miss a couple of final
messages.

We had two folks drop out of the race early and make their own way back
from mile 3, and we had one runner drop out at mile 9.5 who was able to
get transportation back to the finish. I'm not aware of any medical
issues.

We have a number of slots open for the Barns and Blooms race this
Saturday! If you know someone who might be available to help, point them
at the staffing sheet (<https://sites.google.com/view/caararaces>) and
tell them we'd love to have them involved.
