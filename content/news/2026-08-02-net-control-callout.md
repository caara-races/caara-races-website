Hello CAARA members!

The race support team is looking for people who are interested in acting
as net control for our runner safety nets. We'd like to give people the
opportunity to practice communication and coordination skills in a safe
and supported setting. You would help coordinate a team of about 8
people (lead vehicle, trail vehicle, and six checkpoints) to ensure the
safety of runners for the YuKanRun (<https://www.yukanrun.com/>) half
marathons. These races run about once/month, March/April through
December.

If you are interested in taken on this role, we ask that:

- You staff a checkpoint for at least 4 races,
- You shadow an existing net control for 1 race, and
- You're willing to commit to at least three races in the upcoming
  season

For more information, see the CAARA race support website,
<https://sites.google.com/view/caararaces>, and in particular the
"[Guidance for CAARA Race Volunteers][1]" document. If you have any
questions or if you would like to get involved, feel free to contact us
at <races@caara.net>.

Kevin, K1KL & Lars, N1LKS\
CAARA Race Coordinators
