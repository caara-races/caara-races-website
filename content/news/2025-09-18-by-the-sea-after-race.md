Thank you everyone for staffing the By-The Sea race last Sunday.

The weather was gorgeous!

There was a police officer stationed at my location; he closed down one
side of the road for runner safety, which helped a lot -- we had runners
going in both directions along Essex, which also had a fair amount of
car traffic.

We had a hard time hearing net control at this event (lots of static, and
occasional drop outs). We may want to explore different equipment or a more
elevated antenna for future events.
