author: N1LKS

Thank you for your service today at the IPA half marathon! It was
beautiful weather for a race, and while it wasn't the fastest race we've
seen, the spread between the first and last runner wasn't a big as we've
seen at some other races.

There were no medical events today, but we did have some near-misses
involving traffic. We're going to see what we can do about getting some
additional assistance to help specifically with traffic safety for races
that involve busy intersections.

There were no communication challenges today; the K1KKM repeater worked
well for all stations, despite it's somewhat unusual view of the time of
day.

We are always looking for your feedback and suggestions and we'll often
share feedback with the group if it looks like it would benefit
everyone. You can always email us at <races@caara.net>.

Kevin, K1KL & Lars, N1LKS\
CAARA Race Coordinators\
<races@caara.net>
