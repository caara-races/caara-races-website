Great job today team, with many challenges. Your volunteerism is greatly
appreciated.

My observations:

- Our team was in place, on time, and provided needed and good
  communication.
- The race was new for CAARA and for YuKan. There are many lessons to be
  learned. I will be emailing the Race Director with a few recommendations.
  At the top of the list is our Checkpoint 4 at Lafayette St. and Derby St.
  Steve, K8ZBE was at this CP. He expressed his concerns about the
  intersecting traffic at a major roadway 4-way intersection with runners
  going all places across the intersection. I watched for a while and after
  working 35 YuKan races, I can say this was the worst situation I've seen.
  There was a police officer in the middle of the intersection doing his
  best, but he was overwhelmed. I know that the YuKan road manager also
  observed this and had similar conclusions -- it can't be repeated next
  year. 2nd on the list is CP1 at Harbor Ave. and Ocean Ave. Similar but
  less crazy, Police officer did a great job and the situation was under
  control. If I was alone there and did not have a police officer, I would
  have been calling for help. There must be a police officer at this
  intersection. I could not observe the whole course at peak times so if
  you have feedback about safety, please email me <kevinlyonsk1kl@gmail.com>.
- The Salem repeater worked well. It was important not to speak right after
  pushing the PTT. Pausing for a count of 1 helped to avoid clipping of the
  first words.
- We did not need the text group as a back-up but it seemed helpful to take
  some incidental traffic off-line and not tie up the repeater frequency.
  I'm thinking it would be good to have a text group every race. It is
  important to remember that the radio is a priority but checking the
  text group periodically might be helpful.
- It could have rained harder and it could have been colder! 😊
- We need regular reminders that CPs do not refer to course miles, but
  rather to intersections. Only Lead and Trail Vehicles announce miles or
  mile markers. CPs are not at exact mileage!
- We need to remember that there is no station to station calling (direct)
  unless at the direction of Net Control or with permission.
- CP stations had to have been cold -- and staying out there was not fun.
  Thanks to all -- the weather will get warmer (right?).
- Race course adjustments for next year's Marblehead race are needed, but
  we can only make recommendations to YuKan. They are historically good
  listeners.

If you have any feedback on the race, safety or otherwise, or any facet
that we should try to improve, please share. I revise the Volunteer
Guidance after almost every race.

Again, thanks for your service to CAARA, YuKan, and the road race
community.

Kevin, K1KL
